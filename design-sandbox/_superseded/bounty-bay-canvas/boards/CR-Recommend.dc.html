Why A. It is the only route that passes silhouette, face and small size together, and a negotiation game needs all three: you read the other person’s face at avatar size, mid-match, under time pressure. Each character’s profession shows in their costume and prop, so Route A also passes role and memory.

Borrow from B: one unexpected species per district (Vesperine the moth, Hogshead the walrus) and bold one-shape silhouettes. Borrow from C: tailoring, restraint and age range (Marigold at 70, Old Mossback). No character is a “random animal in a jacket”: the trade justifies every species choice.

Guardrails for A: no oversized eyes by default (the lids carry expression), no monocles, no pirate hats, no circular portraits, no two characters sharing a head shape, and no saturated ember, violet or green on costumes.
