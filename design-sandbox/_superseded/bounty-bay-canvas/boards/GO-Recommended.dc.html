Why A over B and C. The Closer is the most socially challenging: B is imposing but slow, which suits a boss or event opponent; C is fun but reads as a trickster, not a peer who wants your money. A looks like he can out-negotiate you and enjoys it, which is the emotion the match needs. B and C are kept as future cast members (Old Mossback’s rival, a Saltmarket street dealer).
