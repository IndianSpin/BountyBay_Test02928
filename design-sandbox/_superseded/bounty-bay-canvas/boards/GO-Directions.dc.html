C1 · 2 — Three redesign directions

Same otter, three different people across the table

A · The Closer

Recommended
Luxury broker, 40s

Shape language

Elegant-sharp: pointed collar, triangle lapels, long curled tail

Sleek, leaning in, too many rings. He smiles with one side of his face and has already priced you. Signature: coin display + red ledger.

B · The Magnate

Strong alternative
Old-money collector, 70s

Shape language

Heavy-round: stole + belly + cane vertical

Portly, slow, imperious. Velvet coat, fur stole, half-moon reading glasses (not a monocle), gold-topped cane. Never raises his voice; never concedes quickly.

C · The Hustler

Explored
Dock-side dealer, 20s

Shape language

Compact-angular: cap brim, scarf tail, flipped coin

Flat cap, rolled ochre jacket, striped scarf, flips a coin while you think. Fast, cheeky, grins with the gold tooth. Charming and a little dangerous.
