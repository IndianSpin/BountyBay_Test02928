4 — Gesture sheet

Eight signature gestures built on the shared hand system

Gavel strike

Decisive close on a settled price.

Warning tap

One tap of the gavel: reconsider.

Point the wing

Directs the challenge straight at you.

Cup a wing to the ear

Wants to hear more before ruling.

SOLD slam

The gavel comes down hard: big win.

Dismissive flick

Brushes a weak offer off the block.

Flat unimpressed shrug

Registers a tiny concession, barely.

Beckon for rematch

Calls the next lot to the block.
