C2 · 10 — Rationale

What changed and why

BodyHe was a head on a stand because nothing connected the head, arms and legs. A neck column, a shoulder yoke and a pear torso make one mass; shorter, thicker legs and the tail on the floor give him weight.
FacePremium expression comes from control, not size. Per-eye lids, a real brow ridge and a cheek that moves with the mouth let him be skeptical, fake-friendly or irritated without cartoon exaggeration.
HandsThe ideas were right but the drawing was noisy. Silhouette-first construction (one knuckle roll, one shape per finger, two rings) keeps every gesture readable at match size.
MaterialsEach material now has its own light response: matte wool, sheen silk, banded metal, fibrous paper, pebbled leather, stranded fur. That removes the clip-art look without going realistic.
Line + finishOne finish layer (heavier outer line, rim light, grain) and material-coloured inner lines replace uniform vector strokes.
SceneThe wharf was repainted in the same language and the same light directions, and the character receives the scene’s light, so he stands in the stall instead of on top of it.
AnimationEvery pose and face is a set of numbers on one rig, so ten states × three keys already exist and in-betweens are interpolation. Timings match the juice spec.
