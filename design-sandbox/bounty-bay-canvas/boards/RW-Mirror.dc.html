RW2 · The loss mirror

Losses get the same choreography, told straight

A reward system that only speaks when you win teaches players to distrust it. Every gain above has a mirror with the same beats, the same length and the same honesty.

PairRule
Her big concession ↔ my big concessionSame heavy slide and knock for both sides. My own big step is not hidden or softened.
Deal ↔ no dealSame length, same freeze. No deal dims the room instead of warming it; the showdown still happens and shows the zone we missed.
Rating gain ↔ rating loss−14 counts at the same speed and size as +18. The division plaque never teases a demotion; if a demotion rule exists (PDR-13) it gets the same single banner as a promotion.
Share of the dealIf I took 37%, the sentence says so (“You took 37% of the deal”). No reframing as a win.
Clock costThe multiplier cost is always shown next to the bounty — the clock is part of the result, not a footnote.
TimeoutNamed as TIMED OUT, never as a walk-away; no extra shaming beat.
What never gets juiceA bluff, a lie, a claim, a streak of losses, a near miss. The game does not dramatise things that are not true or not earned.
