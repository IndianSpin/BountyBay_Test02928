Conversation feels large; the chat UI stays small

The latest line from each person lives in the scene as a spoken bubble. The composer is one row: a text field plus the quick lines. History is a drawer. On my turn, typing shows a small “talking” marker on my clock — because talking costs me time, and they can see it.

Numbers in talk never look like offers. “He's at 80” is plain text in a bubble; 84 is a violet plaque on the rail. Distinguishable by shape and position, not colour alone (docs/09 accessibility).
