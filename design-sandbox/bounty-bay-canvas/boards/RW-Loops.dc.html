RW0 · Open loops

Every event hands you to the next one

Read left to right, top to bottom: each event closes one question and opens exactly one more. The last loop (a person waiting on you, a review, one more qualifying deal) is what brings you back to The Bay tomorrow. Events 08–14 repeat inside the table; 16/17 replace 15 when there is no deal.
