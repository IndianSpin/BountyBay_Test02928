C2 · 4 — Negotiation gesture sheet

Eight standard gestures, one hand system

Ledger holdplays on: idle, listening

Default. The red ledger rides at his hip, fingers curled over the spine. It is his memory: every price you ever paid.

One-finger warningplays on: your low offer lands

Ringed index up at shoulder height, the rest folded into one knuckle roll. “Not so fast.”

Open-palm invitationplays on: crossed offers, speaking

Palm up, arm out to the side, fingers together. Offers you the seal: “After you.”

Ring-hand flourishplays on: large concession, idle accent

Back of the hand at cheek height, fingers fanned with graded curl so all three rings catch the light.

Dismissive flickplays on: weak offer (alt), no deal

Hand flicked outward at hip height, fingers splayed back, a swoosh trail. The offer is brushed off the table.

Seal-the-dealplays on: acceptance

Fist around the wax-seal stamp, pressed onto the contract; the other palm flat to hold the paper.

“Not enough”plays on: weak offer

Thumb and index held a hair apart at eye level, spark ticks mark the gap. The size of your concession.

Rematch beckonplays on: rematch

Palm up, index curling toward himself, arm forward. A personal challenge.
