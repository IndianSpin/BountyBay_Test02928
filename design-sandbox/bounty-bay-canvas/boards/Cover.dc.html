Product design package · v2 (world-first) · Bounty Bay Design System v1 + v1.1 amendment Bounty Bay

The world is playful.
The numbers are ruthless.

A complete consumer design for a competitive 1v1 negotiation game — from first click to the hundredth rematch. v2 makes Bounty Bay a place: the market, your opponent and the object you’re fighting over carry the screen; interface floats on top, and only four things stay on during a deal — their offer, your offer, your secret limit, whose turn.

How to read this canvas

Pages run in build priority. 00 architecture → 01 flows → 02–05 live match, voice, result (max fidelity) → 06 onboarding → 07–15 secondary surfaces → 16 edge states → 17 component states → 18 motion + playable prototype → 19 copy & handoff.

Red stickies mark Product decision required — behaviour the brief leaves open. The screens show a sensible default so nothing is blank, but the default is not a decision.
