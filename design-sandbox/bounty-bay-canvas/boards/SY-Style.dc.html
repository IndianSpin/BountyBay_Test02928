K · Style specification

Premium casual, midcore: ink, cel and warm rim

AspectRule
ProportionAnimals 2.6–3.2 heads; humans 3–3.4 heads; head scaled 1.2–1.34× on the rig. Silhouette over anatomy.
ExaggerationOne exaggerated feature per character (tail, crest, belly, hat, shell). Everything else stays calm.
MaterialsBrass, velvet, wax, enamel, paper, wood, stone. Each has one highlight rule (brass = soft band, enamel = hard dot).
LightingKey: warm, upper-right. Rim: #FFD98A on the lit edge, 7 px offset. Scene light tints the table: ember (me) / violet (them) / green (deal).
TextureNone on characters at this stage; the paint pass may add a 4–6% grain. Backgrounds: flat + gradient haze.
OutlineInk #2A1A2E. 5 px characters and foreground, 3 px midground, none in the background. Round joins.
FaceBuilt from the face kit (A4). Features sit in the lower 60% of the head; the forehead carries brows.
EyesAlmond, species-appropriate size. Lids carry the emotion. Big eyes only when the species demands it (Vesperine).
CostumeProfession first, then status, then personality. Max 3 costume colours + 1 metal. No real-world national dress.
ShadowTwo-tone cel: one shadow shape, lower-left, 20–30% darker and cooler. Ground shadow ellipse, 20% ink.
SaturationCharacters mid-high; world mid; semantic UI highest. No costume uses saturated ember, violet or deal-green.
PerspectiveEye level at the table edge (first person). Characters are drawn frontal-3/4 to face the camera.
Background integrationThe opponent stands in the calm centre against the darkest value; the rim light separates them. Scene key = character key.
