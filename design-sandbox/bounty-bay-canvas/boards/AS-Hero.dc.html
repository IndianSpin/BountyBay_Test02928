Value cues: every hero asset gets all six

  • Material truth: brass has thickness, glass has glare, paper has edges
  • A jewel moment: one specular pop (ruby, cylinder cap, illuminated letter)
  • Presentation: cushion, stand or open spread. Never floating in space
  • Provenance detail: maker’s plate, lot tag, wax seal
  • Warm halo (gold, 55% max) plus at most four sparkles
  • Rim light upper-right, matching the characters
