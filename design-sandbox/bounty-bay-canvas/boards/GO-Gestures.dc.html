C1 · 5 — Negotiation gesture sheet

Hands are where the negotiation shows

Coin display

Thumb + index hold a coin up toward you: “this is what it costs.” His idle signature.

Finger wag

One ringed index up, the rest curled: “not so fast.” Plays on your low offers.

Palm over the offer

Flat palm pressed on the plaque: “this is my number.” Plays when his offer lands.

Theatrical spread

Both palms open, fingers wide: “what more do you want from me?”

Drumming

Fingers drum the plaque while he waits for you. Slow, then faster as your clock runs down.

Rematch point

Finger-gun straight at you, gold tooth out. The rematch challenge.

Counting

Two fingers up: “two more and we have a deal.”

Shrug

Palms down, shoulders up: “your loss.” Plays on no deal.

Clenched

Both hands clenched at the chest when the gap stays wide on his clock.

Whisker twirl

Leans back and twirls the waxed whisker. Plays when he is ahead.

Hands are built from a palm plus tapered three-segment fingers (knuckle creases, claws), so any gesture is a new set of finger angles, not a new drawing. Rings sit on index, ring (signet) and pinky, so the hand silhouette always says “money”.
