Priority, top to bottom

PRIMARY ranked human play → SOCIAL (letters, live tables) → DAILY → COMPETITION → PRACTICE → PERSONAL. Physical objects carry the meaning, but every object also has a plain label: clarity beats diegetic cleverness.
