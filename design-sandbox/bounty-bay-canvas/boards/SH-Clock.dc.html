7:00 each (PDR-01). Only the face of the player who must make a formal decision runs. The clock is quiet while time is plentiful, louder when it is low, and tense when it is critical — and it never moves a control out from under a thumb.

PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED — what happens at 0:00

A — Attributed no-deal (canon today)

NO_DEAL · TIMED_OUT · attributed to the player at 0:00 · zero bounty both. Clear, already built, harsh on the other player who did nothing wrong.

B — Auto-accept the standing offer

The deal closes at the opponent’s last offer. Rewards the waiting player; changes GR-024 and invites stalling toward a good standing offer.

C — Sudden death, then A

At 0:00 each further decision gets a short fixed allowance (e.g. 10 s). Keeps deals alive; adds a second clock rule to explain.

Rating treatment is open in every option (OQ-005, the timeout note in docs/14). Nothing in the storyboard reaches 0:00.
