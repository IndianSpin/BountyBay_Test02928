A · World art direction

Bounty Bay is a fantastical global marketplace

A harbour city where every stall trades something astonishing: instruments that point at wishes, engines that race comets, codices bound in starlight, the right to name a new island. The world supports the scenario, and the scenario supports the negotiation. Five districts give us five moods, five material palettes and five families of goods.

1

A crossroads market, not a pirate cove

Traders from invented places meet at the Bay. Ships and harbours are geography, not costume: no skulls, eyepatches, tricorns or treasure maps with an X.

2

Every stall is a profession

Each district has a trade (navigation, machines, archives, auctions, curios) and every scenario is sold at the stall that fits it.

3

Invented, never borrowed

No real flags, national dress or sacred symbols. Cultures are invented from trade and climate, so no player sees themselves as a costume.

4

Grown-up warmth

Premium casual / midcore. The humour comes from character and tells, not slapstick. Nothing reads as a children’s app, a crypto mascot or a furry avatar.

5

The table is the stage

Everything that matters happens across one table: the thing for sale, the person across from you, and the objects you trade with.
