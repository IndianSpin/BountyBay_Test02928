PV3 · K — Game-juice system

Human significance sets the volume

Every level is triggered by something a person did, and every trigger is a domain event computed from public data (docs/09: the server stays authoritative; the client never decides what was “large” or “close”). Time pressure keeps its canonical tiers (NORMAL / LOW / CRITICAL, GR-024) and never escalates past them.
