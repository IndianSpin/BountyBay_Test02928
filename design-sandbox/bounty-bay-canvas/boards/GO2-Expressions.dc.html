C2 · 3 — Facial expression sheet

Eight target expressions, driven by lids, brows and the near mouth corner

AmusedYour offer is cute.

Lids 60/50 %, lower lids pushed up by the cheek, near brow lifts, half-open grin, gold tooth shows

Skeptical“That number? Really?”

Asymmetric: near eye wide under a brow raised 44 px, far eye squinted to 40 %; lips pressed, far corner pulled up

Fake-friendlySmiles with the mouth only.

Eyes fully open with no cheek push (the tell), inner brows up; wide symmetrical smile, teeth

Pleased by your concessionYou moved; he savours it.

Happy-closed arcs, cheek pushed high, strong one-sided smirk into the cheek, chin up 10°

UnimpressedYour step was tiny.

Flat lids at 34/30 %, brows level, mouth pressed and the near corner pulled down, whiskers drop

Deal-ready“Shall we?”

Focused: lids at 70 % with a firm lower lid, brows low and level, small closed smile, head forward

TriumphantDeal closed in his favour.

Happy-closed eyes, brows high, mouth fully open with the gold tooth, head thrown back 14°

IrritatedHis clock, your hold.

Far eye twitches to 40 %, brows hard down and in, nose wrinkle, lip press + sneer (one canine), ears back

Lids (per eye)Upper lid height and lower-lid push are set per eye, so skeptical and irritated can be asymmetric. The lid casts a shadow onto the eyeball; closed-happy is an arch, not a line
BrowsTapered fur tufts with a hard outer corner and a soft brow-ridge shadow underneath: the socket has depth. Raise ±44 px, angle ±44°
MouthNear corner carries the attitude (curls into the cheek); far corner stays under the nose. Extra controls: lip press, sneer (one canine), open with gold tooth
Cheek ↔ mouthThe smile deepens the nasolabial fold and lifts the whisker pad and the lower lid together, so a real smile is visible in the eyes
MuzzleNose wrinkle for irritation; whisker perk up for interest and down for contempt
