LayerRule
1 BackgroundNo line, 30–45% haze toward the sky colour, lowest saturation. Sky, far coast, city.
2 Midground3 px ink, shaded flat. The stall itself: racks, canopy, charts. Tells you the trade.
3 Opponent5 px ink, cel shade + rim. Stands in the calm centre (x 36–75%) against the darkest, simplest area.
4 Asset5 px ink, halo, specular pop. On the runner, left of centre, never covering the face.
5 Table + handsFirst person. My sleeves in ember, their runner in violet. Every UI object sits here.
6 Foreground frame5 px ink, darker values: posts, rope, girders, stone. Frames the stage and gives depth.
7 AtmosphereFireflies, rain, dust, god-rays. Max 8% opacity over the centre; never over numbers.
