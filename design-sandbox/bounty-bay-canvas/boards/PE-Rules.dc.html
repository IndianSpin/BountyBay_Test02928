PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED · Expressions must not leak limits

If an opponent’s face reacted to MY offer relative to THEIR private limit (e.g. “surprised” because my bid is above their limit), it would reveal the limit before the result. Rule proposed: expression triggers read only public data (offers, steps, clocks). Needs sign-off from game design.

PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED · Human opponents: automatic or chosen?

Option 1: all expressions automatic from public events (recommended, zero input cost, cannot be used to bluff). Option 2: add 4 player-chosen emotes (smug, pleased, thinking, rematch) with a cooldown. Option 2 adds a bluffing channel and a moderation surface.

Motion rules

  • State change = 180 ms ease-out pose blend + 2-frame face swap, never a hard cut
  • One reaction per event; reactions queue, and none plays during my composing
  • Opponent idle breathing never stops, even while waiting
  • Reduced motion: pose swaps only, no FX, no speed lines
