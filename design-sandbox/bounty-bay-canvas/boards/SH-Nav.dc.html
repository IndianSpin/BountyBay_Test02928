SH3 · 9 — Navigation model

Three hubs and one tunnel

Outside a match there are three places and they never change: THE BAY, PLAY, ME. From the moment a person is found until the result is dealt with, the player is in the tunnel: no hubs, no badges, nothing that pulls attention away from the person across the table.
