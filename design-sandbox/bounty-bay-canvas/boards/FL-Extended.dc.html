Re-entry rules

Closing the app mid-match resumes the match on next open (reconnect state). Closing during result resumes at the final result frame, not the reveal start. A friend link opened by the sender shows the waiting-room, not a second challenge.

Where AI can appear

Only: tutorial (The House), Practice, no-human-found fallback, and Daily Deal if D9 resolves to AI. Each entry point states “AI opponent” before the match starts, and the AI pill stays on the opponent seat throughout.
