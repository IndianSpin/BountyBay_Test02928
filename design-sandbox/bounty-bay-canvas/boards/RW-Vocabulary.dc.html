Seven beats for every event that matters

Each important event is choreographed as the same seven beats. The row below is the deal at 74 from the reference match (BOB vs KESTREL, THE RUBY COMPASS). The other 24 events are specified on RW1 and RW2 with the same beats, so engineering can build one sequencer, not 25 bespoke animations.

ANTICIPATION
What tells me something is coming?
Hold-to-accept ring fills around ACCEPT 74; the room hushes.
ACTION
What did I / the opponent do?
I accept 74.
IMPACT
What physically changes?
Freeze · plaques lock green · seal knocks · clocks stop · controls leave.
REVEAL
What new information appears?
Her limit turns over: 59. The zone is 41.
REWARD
What did I gain / lose?
I took 26 of 41 (63%) · ×84% bounty · +18 → MERCHANT II.
PERSISTENCE
What stays changed afterwards?
Ledger line, division plaque, the 74 in the review, 4 / 5 on the board.
NEXT OPEN LOOP
What now pulls me forward?
She wants the other side — SWAP SIDES, or her letter on The Bay.

Nine rules every choreography obeys

RuleWhat it means here
Earned by a personEvery beat is caused by something a person did (or a server fact about it). A human’s avatar performs only what the human did (PV0).
Anticipation is informationOnly real pending states build suspense: a clock running, a card face-down, a seal unbroken. No fake near-misses, no “almost there”.
Impact is physicalObjects move: plaques slide, chips leave, seals knock, pins push, flags drop. No toasts, no floating +XP text.
Reveal once, in orderEach fact appears once, in the order a player asks for it; skippable after the first beat.
Rewards are trueThe reward is exactly the real change. Losses are shown at the same size and speed as gains; the clock’s cost is never hidden.
Claims get zero volumeTalk, bluffs and lies never trigger juice. The system never reacts to truth (PV3 level 0).
Everything leaves a traceEvery reward persists somewhere you can see later: the rail, the table, the ledger, a letter, the board, the profile.
One open loop at a timeEach event ends by pointing at exactly one next thing. One gold pull, never three.
Volume = human significancePV3 ladder 0–10. Only level 10 (promotion, rivalry lead change, series won) may take the whole screen. No confetti (docs/09). Every beat has a reduced-motion equivalent.
