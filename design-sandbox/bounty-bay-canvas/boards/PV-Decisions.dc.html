19 mechanics design can't decide alone

Every storyboard draws a default so nothing is blank. A default is not a decision. Each item points to the rule or open question it touches.

Mechanics

Question · why it mattersDefault drawn
PD-1Is there a formal HOLD / PASS action?
A pass would change turn structure and the anti-stalling design.
No — hold = talk while your clock burns (DEC-026).
PD-2Decision-time budget for social play
Talking burns your clock; too short and nobody talks (OQ-017).
90 s per player (test default) ⇒ ≤ 3:00 per match.
PD-3Can I see the other player's chips?
Visible chips turn “can they still move?” into a readable fact.
Hidden (canvas default).
PD-4Is the dossier read before the clock starts?
Otherwise the first mover is punished for reading.
Yes, ~10 s untimed read after the role reveal.
PD-5Verified reveal: how many, what cost?
Too cheap and every fact gets shown; too dear and nobody shows (OQ-020).
One per match, free; formal action.

Showdown & feedback

Question · why it mattersDefault drawn
PD-6Reveal unrevealed dossier facts at the showdown?
The biggest bluff payoff — and a privacy/meta-gaming question (OQ-019).
Drawn as a variant (her “other buyer” line flips).
PD-7“Close” and “large concession” thresholds
Must be domain-computed from public offers only, never from limits.
Close = public gap ≤ 20% of opening gap; large = step ≥ 2× that player's previous step.
PD-8Showdown pacing after the novelty
Veterans play hundreds of matches; a long reveal becomes a tax.
Full ~7 s for early matches, ~2.5 s later, tap to skip.
