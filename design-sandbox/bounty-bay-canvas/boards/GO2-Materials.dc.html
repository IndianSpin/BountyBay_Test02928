Five materials that read differently at a glance

4 · + scene light
Finish layerOne filter over the whole figure: a heavier outer silhouette line (interior lines stay thin and coloured), a warm rim light from the upper right, and a fine grain
Line qualityMaterial-coloured lines, never black: dark brown on fur, deep teal on wool, brass on metal. Weight varies: outer 6–7 px, inner 2.5–3.5 px
Scene lightIn a scene the figure receives that scene’s lights through a mask of itself: lantern key, coral fill, table bounce, cool falloff at the bottom
Fur

Warm radial gradient with sub-surface orange at the edges; strand groups (light on top, dark in the shadows); small tufts break the jaw and skull outline

Wool coat

Matte: broad soft light, no sharp spec. A diagonal twill texture; folds drawn as shadow + lit-ridge pairs

Silk

Cravat and vest: a narrow hard sheen band plus the brocade pattern. The only cloth with a sharp highlight

Metal

Banded gold gradient (light → dark core → reflected light) with a hard white specular: cuffs, rings, buttons, coin, stamp

Paper

Off-white with fibre noise, ruled ink lines, a folded corner and a thin brown edge line

Leather

Deep red with pebble grain, stitched border, a worn highlight on the corners; the loafers get a patent sheen instead
