OS · 3 — DESIGN RATIONALE

Why the opening screen looks like this

Host, not lineupGoldenOtter fronts the screen alone — he is the benchmark character and the one every player meets in the tutorial. A full cast grid up front would read as a character-select screen, which this game does not have (opponents are matched, never picked).
Cast is a teaser, not a menuThe avatar strip is a promise ("here is who you'll face"), not a navigation control — none of the seven are tappable here. Clicking a face here would imply pick-your-opponent, which breaks matchmaking's fairness story.
One dominant actionPLAY NOW is the only gold button on the screen. Watch a match and Create account are visually secondary so a returning player's eye still lands on one place.
Scene over chromeReuses the same Lantern Wharf environment and GoldenOtter render built for the live-match screen (C2 · 2), so the title screen and the first real match feel like the same place, not a separate marketing wrapper.
Live social proofThe "Live · N deals in progress" pill is the only stat on the screen — it signals a real, populated game world without competing with the wordmark for attention.
Mobile: scene shrinks, decision stays putOn mobile the wharf art becomes a top band and the CTA stack drops onto a solid parchment-dark panel so PLAY NOW is always in the thumb zone, never floating over busy art.
