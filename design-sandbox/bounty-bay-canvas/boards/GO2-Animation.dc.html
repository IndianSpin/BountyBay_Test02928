C2 · 8 — Animation key-pose sheet

Ten states, each a start / middle / end on one rig

Every frame below is generated from the same rig (pose = arm angles, lean, head tilt, expression values), so in-betweens are interpolations of numbers, not new drawings. Layers for export: tail · coat tails · legs · far arm · torso · head (lids, brows, mouth, whiskers as separate parts) · near arm · props.
