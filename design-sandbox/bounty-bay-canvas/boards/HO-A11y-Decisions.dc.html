J · Accessibility, animation intent, open decisions

Before you build

Accessibility

• Every state is carried by shape + position + text, never colour alone (ember/violet also differ by side of table and label).
• Live region announces: “Their offer 74, was 78. Your move.” / “Deal possible.” / “Deal at 74.”
• Hold-to-accept has a keyboard equivalent (Enter held 600 ms, or Enter then confirm).
• Text on scenes sits on ≥ 80% opaque plates; body text ≥ 4.5:1.
• Reduced motion honoured everywhere; no flashing above 3 Hz.
• Screen-reader names for busts: “GOLDENOTTER, opponent, thinking”.

Animation intent

Motion explains cause (see 18 · Motion). Durations 150–600 ms; result beats ≤ 8 s total and skippable. Opponent poses switch on events (offer received → offer pose 1.5 s → idle; their turn → thinking; voice activity → speaking).
