Pacing rules

  • Full sequence ≈ 7 s for a player's early matches; ≈ 2.5 s compressed later (beats 3–7 merge) — PD-8.
  • Tap anywhere skips to beat 8. Nothing is withheld: the result is already final on the server.
  • Reduced motion: the beats become a crossfade of stills; the pause becomes a 300 ms fade; no count-up.
  • No-deal runs beats 3–6, then the truthful gap (DEADLOCK / MISSED OPPORTUNITY / NO ZOPA, GR-026). No capture number.
  • No confetti, no coins, no jackpot sounds (docs/09). The drama is the cards, not the particles.
