Five hard questions, answered honestly

Does it still feel too childlike?Mostly noHead is 22% of height (was ~45%), adult tailoring, narrow heavy-lidded eyes, gold tooth. Residual softness: the round skull and big cream muzzle are still friendly. That is kept on purpose (one soft element), but a painter should sharpen the brow ridge further.
Does it still feel generic?NoNothing about him is a default: pointed collar, waxed whisker, coin display, gold tooth, ledger, wax-seal fob. Each detail is tied to how he negotiates.
Game opponent or mascot?Opponent3/4 stance toward you, eye contact from under heavy lids, an asymmetric smirk and a gesture aimed at you (coin, finger wag, finger-gun). Mascots pose for the viewer; he negotiates with you.
Remembered after one match?Likely“The smug otter with the rings and the gold tooth who flashes a coin at you.” Three hooks at three sizes: silhouette (collar + tail), face (smirk + tooth), hand (coin + rings).
Does the rendering still look like cheap flat vector?Much less, not fullySoft volume, material separation, rim light and fur strands remove the clip-art feel. It is still clean vector: final production needs a painter pass (texture, hand-drawn line weight). Also: the environments are now flatter than him. Upgrade them to the same shading before shipping.
