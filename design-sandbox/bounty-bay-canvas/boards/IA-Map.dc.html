A · Information architecture (v2)

Bay. PLAY. Me.

Three persistent places. The player thinks “what am I doing next?”, not “which section am I in?”. Daily Deal, rivals and events are things in the Bay; rating, divisions and leaderboards are part of Me; every mode is behind PLAY and only appears when it is relevant.

Bay

The harbour. Answers “what next?” with one objective and one big button.

  • PLAY RANKED — the dominant object
  • One objective chip (placement / division / best)
  • Rival standing on the dock (only if a rematch is pending)
  • Today’s Deal as a glowing bottle on a stall
  • Live event lantern (only while live)

PLAY

Gold button (desktop top-right, mobile centre tab). A short sheet, never a menu wall.

  • Always: RANKED · TODAY’S DEAL · PRACTICE
  • “Right now” (only when real): event check-in, Deal Night invite, promoted rotating mode
  • Challenge a friend — unlocked after first human deal

Me

Your record and your standing.

  • Rating, division, next threshold
  • Leaderboards (global · week · Daily)
  • History → result → replay
  • Rivals · play style
  • Settings, blocked players
