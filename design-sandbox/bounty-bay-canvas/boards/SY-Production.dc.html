K · Production rules

How the art system is built and extended

RigEach character = one function over the shared rig: body plan + costume + prop + head. 15 states (14 + seller) from a shared pose library with per-character overrides.
Sprite formatOne SVG sheet per character, 600×900 cells side by side, ground at y 872, head centre documented. Crops (portrait, bust, avatar, icon) are coordinates, not files.
Scenes1600×1000 SVG, calm centre x 36–75%. Table foreground is a separate layer (desktop 1600×440, mobile 780×900) so the opponent sits between the two.
AnimationIdle breathing (scale-y 1.00–1.012, 3 s), blink, 180 ms pose blend, 2-frame face swap, FX layer on top. Reduced motion = swaps only.
Adding a character1 concept line + trade · 2 silhouette test vs cast · 3 face test at 64/96 px · 4 all 15 states · 5 no semantic hues · 6 review against the reject list.
AccessibilityThe UI never relies on character expression: every state also has text, light and position. Numbers are real text on objects, never baked into art.
