PV1 · B — Core PvP loop

From “opponent found” to “swap sides” in about three minutes

~4 sMATCH FOUNDOpponent first: handle, avatar, rating, one social fact (first deal / rivalry).
~10 sTHE DEAL + MY ROLEAsset, role, sealed limit, two dossier facts. Untimed read (PD-4).
≤ 3:00THE TABLETalk · move · hold · show · accept · walk. One clock at a time.
0.6 sACCEPTFreeze. Plaques lock. Controls leave.
~7 s → 2.5 sSHOWDOWNLimits turn over, ZOPA, settlement, capture %, one true insight.
~3 sTHE SCORERivalry tally, series, rating. One next hook only.
10 s windowSWAP SIDESBoth opt in: same asset, fresh limits, roles flipped. Or rematch, or new opponent.
“ONE MORE DEAL.” — the same person, the other side

Inside THE TABLE — on my turn I choose one of six; on theirs, I read

TALK
chat · quick line · pitch
free — can lie
MOVE
a new offer (the only thing that passes the turn)
chips · irreversible
HOLD
stay on your turn and talk
your bounty, in public
SHOW
verified reveal
a formal action (PD-5)
ACCEPT
their standing offer
ends the match
WALK
no deal
zero for both

Only MOVE, ACCEPT and WALK change the turn or end the match (GR-007, GR-010, GR-012). TALK is allowed on either turn (GR-013). There is no pass (PD-1).

On their turn I watch four things

  • their clock (thinking, holding, burning)
  • their words (and whether their moves agree)
  • the size and speed of their steps (the trail)
  • their emotes — chosen, never generated

Everything I read is public and true. What it means is my job.

SECONDS

Offer lands, step size, hold, the showdown's card turns.

SESSIONS

Swap sides, series, “settle the score”, a personal best to beat.

DAYS

Rival challenges waiting, Daily Deal, scheduled Live Tables (later pass).
