Documented amendment to Design System v1

Playful world, hard numbers — v1.1

What changed and why

1 · Environment palette added. v1 had only chrome surfaces (world/elevated/overlay). Scenes now use a dusk palette (below) so the product is lit at golden hour, not midnight. Seat semantics are untouched: ember = you, violet = them, gold = value, teal = chips, green = deal.

2 · Parchment reserved for physical objects. Offer plaques, the offer slip, the result scroll, share cards. Received chat bubbles move from parchment to violet-ink; sent to ember-ink.

3 · Silhouettes by meaning. Rounded rectangles are for utility chrome only. Game objects get their own shapes: chamfered plaques, notched wax-seal cards, forked banners, coin tokens, chip stacks, scroll certificates, hex name-signs.

4 · Display type used sparingly. Baloo 2 for banners, names and one headline per screen; never for anything a player must compare. Numbers stay Space Grotesk.

5 · Glow still means reward. Additional allowed glows: the green crossing line and the gold “close” line on the rail — both are state, not decoration.
