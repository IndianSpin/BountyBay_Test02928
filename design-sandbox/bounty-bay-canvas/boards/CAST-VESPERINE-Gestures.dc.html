4 — Gesture sheet

Eight signature gestures built on the shared hand system

Hand over the lantern

Stillness; the flame barely moves.

Hands folded

Patient, giving nothing away.

Finger to lips

A secret worth paying for.

Wing opens

Reveals the curio at last.

Hovering pass

A slow hand drifts over the goods.

Beckons closer

Draws you in to look closer.

Withdraws

Pulls the offer back off the table.

Offers the light

Extends the lantern toward you.
