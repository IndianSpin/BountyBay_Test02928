J1 · The juice system

Numbers must cause physical events in the game world

Every number change is an event that the whole scene listens to: the rail, the objects, the chips, the light, the opponent, the asset, the stall, the sound and the haptics all react, offset by a few milliseconds so it reads as one physical cause with many consequences, never as widgets animating on their own.

MICRO

MICRO

Ordinary interaction feedback

  • 80–250 ms
  • No particles (at most 1 dust puff)
  • One soft material sound
  • Tick haptic
  • Never shakes the camera
  • Never delays input

e.g. Stepper ticks, turn flips, thinking loops, voice ripples, small concessions

MOMENT

MOMENT

A strategically meaningful state change

  • 300–900 ms
  • ≤ 6 diegetic particles (dust, chips, sparks)
  • One sound phrase, max 2 layers
  • Tap / thud / double
  • No camera shake
  • The next legal action is live from ~50% of the duration

e.g. Offer landing, chip spend, large concession, crossing, walk-away, timeout, rematch

REWARD

REWARD

Deal, result and progression payoff

  • 0.9–4 s staged sequence; each beat ≤ 1.2 s
  • ≤ 12 diegetic particles per beat (coins, sparks)
  • Musical phrase; silence before the peak
  • Heavy, then a roll
  • Max 2 screen-shakes per match (deal stamp, promotion), 6 px / 120 ms
  • Tap anywhere to skip to the end state; numbers never hidden

e.g. Acceptance, limits reveal, surplus split, headline, rating, promotion

One event, many listeners

Stagger offsets make cause → effect readable

Never

  • Particle spam or confetti: every particle is a thing in the world (dust, chips, coins, sparks off brass)
  • Casino or slot-machine effects: no spinning reels, flashing “WIN”, coin fountains without a count, or near-miss teasing
  • Constant screen-shake: two per match at most
  • Effects that cover a number: FX sit behind or beside numbers, never on them
  • Rewards that delay play: in-match effects never block the next legal action; tapping fast-forwards to the end state in 80 ms
  • Emotion that leaks private limits: character reactions read public data only
  • Heat as a new meter: closeness is a climate of the whole scene, not a bar
