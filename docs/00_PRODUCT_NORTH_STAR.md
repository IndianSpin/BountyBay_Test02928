# Product North Star

## Product definition

Bounty Bay is a competitive bargaining game in which two players receive private reservation values, bargain over one numerical settlement, and are objectively evaluated on how much of the available bargaining surplus they capture.

The initial game is **single-issue distributive bargaining**, not a complete model of negotiation. It deliberately focuses on anchoring, concession behavior, patience, risk, opponent reading, settlement discipline, and efficient value capture.

## Initial audience

V1 is for competitive individuals. The first test is consumer behavior: do people voluntarily play, rematch, return, care about results, and invite others?

Students, trainers, business schools, procurement teams, sales organizations, enterprise assessment, and real-asset modes are later opportunities. They must not distort the initial consumer game.

## Core promise

> Can you get the better deal against another person when neither of you knows the other's limit?

The emotional center is consequential competition, not classroom instruction. Learning and analysis are valuable secondary outcomes.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Human PvP is canonical.** The most meaningful competitive result is against another human.
2. **AI is transparent.** AI may solve cold-start liquidity and practice needs but must never masquerade as a human.
3. **Hidden information is real.** A player's reservation value is private and server-protected.
4. **Players create the negotiation.** The engine constrains incentives and legality; humans create persuasion, bluffing, framing, and messages.
5. **Settlement quality dominates.** Clock and concession mechanics shape behavior but must not overwhelm the central question of who captured the bargaining surplus.
6. **No pay-to-win.** Money may later buy tournament entry, analysis, content, or premium experiences, but never better BATNAs, extra clock time, or competitive advantages inside a rated match.
7. **Competitive integrity matters.** Canonical rating must be protected from AI games, async play, collusive friend boosting, and hidden rule changes.
8. **Measure before scaling.** V1 exists to validate repeated play and skill signal before expensive real-money, regulatory, enterprise, or content expansion.
9. **Rules are deterministic.** Authoritative game rules must be explicit, testable, and server-side.
10. **Fantasy is a wrapper, not the value proposition.** Theme creates identity and variety; consequences make the outcome matter.

## Business objective

The company is for-profit. The near-term objective is to find a repeatable consumer loop that can create traffic and later monetize through premium analysis, tournaments/prizes, Real Mode, and organizational products.

A philanthropic arm may exist later, but it is not a V1 design constraint.

## Long-term product possibilities

- Free ranked human bargaining network.
- AI sparring and personalized practice.
- Post-match replay and negotiation analysis.
- Real-asset research and scheduled competitions.
- Cash/sponsored tournaments, subject to legal feasibility.
- Multi-issue scenario generation.
- Education/trainer tooling.
- Team and enterprise benchmarking.
- Negotiation research and AI-agent benchmarking.

These are optional extensions. The company should not assume they are valuable until the core bargaining loop demonstrates retention.
