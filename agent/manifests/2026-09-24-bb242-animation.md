# Context Manifest — BB-242 animation integration

## Objective

Wire the founder's 12 fps WebP sprite sheets (8 characters × 10
clips, grid cells, manifest at /game/anim/manifest.json) into the
live-match pose system: a manifest-driven sprite player animates the
opponent's key states (idle/think/speak/offer/react for
idle/thinking/speaking/offer/smug; offline stays idle); one-shots
play once and hold their last frame per the manifest's holdLast;
reduced motion falls back to the static pose (the current layer).
