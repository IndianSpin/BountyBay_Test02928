# PRODUCT FINDINGS — Bounty Bay QA red team

Product-level observations (not conventional bugs). DAU lens:
ACQUISITION ≠ RETENTION. For each major flow assess:

- COMPREHENSION — objective, my limit, opponent offer, available
  action, what happened: was it understandable?
- MOMENT-TO-MOMENT — did actions feel consequential?
- NEGOTIATION FEEL — negotiating, or entering numbers into a form?
- RESULT — satisfying and understandable?
- NEXT ACTION — compelling reason to rematch / improve / practice /
  return tomorrow?
- FRICTION — where would a normal user quit?
- REPETITION — would the tenth match differ meaningfully from the
  first?
- DAU QUESTIONS — another match now? another session tomorrow?
  meaningful progress? requires another human online? repetitive? an
  unfinished goal? fresh content? social pull?

Evidence-based only. Do not manufacture problems; do not invent major
new features unless asked.
